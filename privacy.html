Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Capsule: Live Widget Videos (“Capsule”) is provided by the developer listed on the App Store page for this app.

Summary

Capsule does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or location. We do not sell your personal information.

Information we process

  • Purchases: If you buy the one-time unlock, Apple processes the payment. We use Apple and RevenueCat only to confirm and restore your purchase entitlement on your devices. We never receive your full payment card details.
  • Media you choose: Videos or photos you pick for Custom Studio stay on your device (including shared App Group storage used by the Home Screen widget). We do not upload your gallery media to our servers.
  • Device data needed to run the app: Standard iOS / StoreKit technical data required to deliver purchases and the widget. We do not use advertising trackers or sell data to data brokers.

How we use information

Only to provide Capsule’s features (live widgets, preview, custom bake) and to unlock / restore premium access you paid for.

Sharing

Purchase verification is handled by Apple and RevenueCat as our service providers. We do not share your content with third parties for advertising.

Retention

Purchase entitlement status is kept as needed to restore your unlock. Media you import remains under your control on your device until you delete it or remove the app.

Children’s privacy

Capsule is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can restore purchases in Settings, revoke photo/video access in iOS Settings, and delete the app to remove local data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer via the email or support link on Capsule’s App Store page.
